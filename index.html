How ratings work

Each member earns a tier based on how many of the tracked bills in their own chamber they sponsor or currently cosponsor. Members can’t cosponsor bills from the other chamber, so House and Senate use different thresholds. Cosponsors who withdrew their names aren’t counted.

TierHouseSenate
Gold6+ bills4+ bills
Silver4–5 bills3 bills
Bronze1–3 bills1–2 bills

Tiers reflect only the bills listed below, so they say nothing about a member’s other work.
