How ratings work

Each member’s rating is the share of tracked bills in their own chamber that they sponsored or currently cosponsor. House members are scored on House bills and senators on Senate bills, because members can’t cosponsor bills from the other chamber. Cosponsors who withdrew their names aren’t counted. Ratings reflect only the bills below, so they say nothing about a member’s other work.
