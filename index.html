    How tiers work

    Each member earns a tier based on how many of the tracked bills in their own chamber they sponsor or currently cosponsor. Members can’t cosponsor bills from the other chamber, so the House and Senate use different thresholds. Cosponsors who withdrew their names aren’t counted. Tiers reflect only the bills listed above, so they say nothing about a member’s other work.
